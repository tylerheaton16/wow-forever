Each crate is bought empty off the auction house, filled with one of the item options shown beside it, then turned in to Dokimi at Durotar Supply & Logistics for Merchant's Favor. Gathered-goods crates (Herbs, Ore, Textiles, Curiosities) are common quality and pay 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 Favor by tier; crafted-goods crates (Ingots, Fabrics, Parts) are uncommon and pay 10 / 20 / 30 / 40. Each turn-in also refunds silver equal to half its Favor number (e.g. a 30-Favor crate pays 15s back), which every calculation below subtracts as a rebate. Expert and Artisan payouts (Favor and silver) are community-reported, not yet officially confirmed. Prices are minimum buyout (market value as fallback, shown in grey). Refresh with ./refresh-prices.sh.
Reputation planner: Notarized Shipping Containers
From Friendly, Kurvelk Shadowhoof sells the Forged Delivery Receipt for 15 Merchant's Favor.
